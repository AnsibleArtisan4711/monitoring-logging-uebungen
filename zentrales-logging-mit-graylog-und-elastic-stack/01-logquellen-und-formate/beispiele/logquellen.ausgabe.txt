PRI=164 facility=20(local4) severity=4(warning) host=<HOST> programm=webapp msg= Platte fast voll
PRI=35 facility=4(auth) severity=3(err) host=<HOST> programm=sshd msg=Failed password for ben from 10.18.0.99
PRI=78 facility=9(cron) severity=6(info) host=<HOST> programm=cron msg= job backup ok

## 5. Priorität (PRI) = Facility × 8 + Severity, mit Python nachgerechnet
  PRI  10 =  1 × 8 + 2  -> user     crit     (RFC 5424)
  PRI 164 = 20 × 8 + 4  -> local4   warning  (RFC 3164)
  PRI  35 =  4 × 8 + 3  -> auth     err      (RFC 5424)
  PRI  78 =  9 × 8 + 6  -> cron     info     (RFC 3164)
